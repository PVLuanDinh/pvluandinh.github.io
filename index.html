What it is

A brain-inspired AI engine that grows its own neurons. When its spiking network cannot solve a problem it adds neurons, passes them through a separate “liquid” reservoir network so they learn how they relate to what it already knows, then runs a sleep cycle that prunes and saves the result to one permanent data store it can recall from.

System design

Two cooperating networks inside a single durable data lake, trained with local, biologically plausible learning rules (STDP, e-prop) instead of backpropagation; a safety layer with an emergency stop on every robot-arm (SO-101) command; and a live dashboard for monitoring and demos.

Research method

Every engine component is tested as a pre-registered experiment with controls and statistical tests before it merges. Built in parallel by a team of AI agents I direct, with peer review and one integrator; the final milestone removes each connection to prove it is needed.

Engineering
